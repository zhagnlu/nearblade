From application to specification

Match the material to the cutting task

Tell us the sheet material, thickness range and production conditions. If an existing knife is chipping, wearing or leaving an unsatisfactory cut, include clear photographs and its known material specification.

The material grade and hardness should be confirmed together with the approved drawing. Supplier reference values are not a substitute for the specification agreed for your order.

Quotation checklist

Send one complete blade specification

  1. Machine manufacturer and model; upper / lower knife quantities.
  2. Dimensioned drawing with hole pattern, edge geometry and critical tolerances.
  3. Sheet material and thickness; existing blade grade and hardness if known.
  4. Required inspection records, matched-set identification and acceptance criteria.
  5. Destination and requested delivery date; confirm edge protection, corrosion protection and packing with the quotation.
